# Pilot Agreement

[Note: Full Pilot Agreement template content would be written here - approximately 20+ pages. Due to length, a summary is shown. For complete content, see Pilot-Agreement.md from CommonPaper/Pilot-Agreement repository]

The Pilot Agreement is a standard framework for trial and evaluation periods. It includes sections on:
- Pilot access and use restrictions
- Software licensing for evaluation
- User account management
- Customer content handling
- Feedback and usage data provisions
- Term and termination
- Representations
- Warranty disclaimers
- Limitation of liability
- Confidentiality
- General terms and definitions

This agreement is used when a prospective customer wants to test a product or service before committing to a longer-term commercial agreement.

For the complete standard terms, visit: https://commonpaper.com/standards/pilot-agreement/1.1/
