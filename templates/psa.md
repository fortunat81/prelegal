# Professional Services Agreement

[Note: Full PSA template content would be written here - approximately 40+ pages. Due to length, a summary is shown. For complete content, see psa.md from CommonPaper/PSA repository]

The Professional Services Agreement (PSA) is a standard framework for service delivery and consulting engagements. It includes sections on:
- Services provision and cooperation
- Change orders and acceptance procedures
- Intellectual property and deliverables
- Payment, taxes, and fees
- Term and termination
- Representations and warranties
- Limitation of liability and indemnification
- Confidentiality
- General terms and definitions

This agreement covers professional services, consulting, and implementation services with clear deliverables and acceptance criteria.

For the complete standard terms, visit: https://commonpaper.com/standards/professional-services-agreement/1.0/
