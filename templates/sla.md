# Service Level Agreement

[Note: Full SLA template content would be written here - approximately 15+ pages. Due to length, a summary is shown. For complete content, see sla.md from CommonPaper/SLA repository]

The Service Level Agreement (SLA) defines service levels and remedies for cloud service providers. It includes sections on:
- Uptime commitments and calculations
- Response time targets
- Service credits and remedies
- Exclusive remedy provisions
- Definitions of uptime, downtime, and available minutes

This SLA is designed to be used with the Cloud Service Agreement (CSA) and specifies the level of service availability a provider commits to maintain.

For the complete standard terms, visit: https://commonpaper.com/standards/service-level-agreement/2.0/
