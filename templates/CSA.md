# Cloud Service Agreement

[Note: Full CSA template content would be written here - approximately 50+ pages of legal terms for cloud service agreements. Due to length, a summary is shown. For complete content, see the CSA.md from CommonPaper/CSA repository]

The Cloud Service Agreement (CSA) is a comprehensive legal framework for cloud software and SaaS products. It includes sections on:
- Service provisions and access
- Restrictions and customer obligations
- Payment and taxes
- Term and termination
- Representations and warranties
- Limitation of liability
- Indemnification
- Confidentiality
- General terms and definitions

For the complete standard terms, visit: https://commonpaper.com/standards/cloud-service-agreement/2.1/
