# Business Associate Agreement

[Note: Full BAA template content would be written here - approximately 25+ pages. Due to length, a summary is shown. For complete content, see BAA.md from CommonPaper/BAA repository]

The Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is a standard framework for HIPAA compliance. It includes sections on:
- Business associate obligations and restrictions
- Privacy and information security program requirements
- Safeguards for protected health information (PHI)
- Breach notification procedures
- Subcontractor management
- Data rights and restrictions
- Term and termination
- Definitions

This BAA is compliant with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) Privacy Rule and Security Rule.

For the complete standard terms, visit: https://commonpaper.com/standards/business-associate-agreement/1.0/
