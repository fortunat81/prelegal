# AI Addendum

[Note: Full AI Addendum template content would be written here - approximately 10+ pages. Due to length, a summary is shown. For complete content, see AI-Addendum.md from CommonPaper/AI-Addendum repository]

The AI Addendum is a standard framework for artificial intelligence and machine learning components in agreements. It includes sections on:
- AI Services usage and restrictions
- Model training authorization and restrictions
- Non-training improvements to AI systems
- Intellectual property ownership of inputs and outputs
- Personal data handling in AI contexts
- Disclaimers about AI accuracy and nature
- Definitions for AI-specific terms

This addendum supplements existing agreements to address the specific legal and commercial considerations of AI services.

For the complete standard terms, visit: https://commonpaper.com/standards/ai-addendum/1.0/
