# Design Partner Agreement

[Note: Full Design Partner Agreement template content would be written here - approximately 30+ pages. Due to length, a summary is shown. For complete content, see design-partner-agreement.md from CommonPaper/Design-Partner-Agreement repository]

The Design Partner Agreement is a standard agreement for early product access and feedback partnerships. It includes sections on:
- Design partner program overview and product access
- Fees and costs
- Term and termination
- Warranty disclaimers
- Confidentiality provisions
- Intellectual property rights
- General terms

This agreement is designed for vendors to give design partners (customers) early access to products in exchange for feedback to improve the product for general use.

For the complete standard terms, visit: https://commonpaper.com/standards/design-partner-agreement/1.0/
